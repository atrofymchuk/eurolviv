## Where to Have a Delicious Breakfast in Lviv: Places for Early and Late Breakfasts

If you’re deciding where to have breakfast in Lviv, choose a spot based on its hours, menu, and service style. For an early breakfast, try Shalom, which opens at 8:00 a.m., or Restauracja Bachevskych, which offers a buffet starting at 8:30 a.m. At ROOF, you can start your day with a leisurely breakfast on the panoramic terrace. French Holidays is convenient because you can order breakfast dishes throughout the day.

Delicious breakfasts in Lviv have long since gone beyond just syrniki, eggs, and oatmeal. Atlas serves hearty dishes, Cheese Bakery combines savory and sweet breakfasts with pastries, and Grand Cafe Leopolis is known for its three-story breakfast towers. Shalom is worth visiting for its shakshuka, hummus, and Israeli-style combinations.

Here’s a selection of Lviv spots for a quick breakfast before a walk, a long brunch, or a morning on the terrace overlooking Rynok Square. And please note that hours, prices, and the seasonal availability of outdoor seating may vary, so it’s best to confirm the details at least one day before your visit.

## Where to Have Breakfast in Lviv: A Selection of Restaurants

### ROOF

At ROOF, breakfast is accompanied by breathtaking views of Lviv. It’s located in the Eurohotel, not far from Lviv’s historic center. Its three-level complex occupies the upper floors of the hotel. From the outdoor seating areas, you can see Lviv’s rooftops, City Hall, and St. George’s Cathedral.

When you visit ROOF, you can order breakfast in Lviv before taking a stroll through the city center or leisurely enjoy a cup of coffee with a view of the city. On a warm day, choose a table on the panoramic terrace to fully appreciate the enchanting views and the soft lighting of Lviv’s rooftops. Keep in mind that the upper level offers more open sky, while the lower terrace is better sheltered from the wind.

- Address: 6A Tershakovtsiv St.
- Hours: daily from 9:00 AM to 12:00 AM.
- Average bill: approximately 350–600 UAH.

### French Holidays

At this spot, the breakfast menu stays on the menu even after lunch. Many restaurants and cafes in Lviv offer late breakfasts, but at French Holidays, they’re served all day long. You can stop by in the evening after a long walk and still order pancakes with cheese and crème brûlée sauce or eggs Benedict with salmon.

Among the more interesting savory options is polenta with wild mushrooms, a poached egg, and homemade brynza. Alongside French pastries, the menu features hearty dishes with eggs and signature sauces. You can stop by for morning coffee or come back closer to lunchtime for a late brunch.

- Address: 1 Rudansky St. / 3 Shevchenko Ave.
- Hours: daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM; breakfast is served all day.
- Average bill: approximately 250–500 UAH.

### Atlas

Atlas is located in a historic stone building on Rynok Square. Inside, the vaulted ceilings, wooden paneling, old documents, and decorative details have been preserved, evoking the atmosphere of Galician restaurants from the early 20th century. At the tables by the windows, you can enjoy breakfast with a view of Rynok Square and watch the morning hustle and bustle in the city center.

The menu features an English breakfast, shakshuka, poached eggs with tuna, and croissants with roast beef or salmon. For a sweet start to the morning, you can order syrniki with cherry jam or caramel. The English breakfast and egg dishes are served in generous portions.

- Address: 45 Rynok Square.
- Hours: daily from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM; breakfast served from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- Average bill: approximately 195–375 UAH.

### Restauracja Bachevskych

Breakfast at Bachevskych differs from the usual à la carte ordering. Guests are served a buffet featuring cold cuts and cheeses, vegetables, salads, egg dishes, fruit, desserts, and pastries. Depending on the current offering, breakfast includes coffee and a glass of sparkling wine or a stronger beverage.

The all-you-can-eat breakfast is served in a green conservatory with a veranda. It’s easy to linger here longer than planned: first grab some eggs and appetizers, then go back for pancakes and dessert. The place is extremely popular, so there’s often a line at the entrance in the morning.

- Address: 8 Shevska St.
- Hours: daily from 8:30 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.; breakfast is served from 8:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m.
- Average bill: approximately 450 UAH for the breakfast menu.

### Cheese Bakery

People choose Cheese Bakery when they want to combine breakfast with dessert and not pretend that the cheesecake is “for later.” The chain specializes in pastries, cheesecakes, and breakfast dishes.

You can start with scrambled eggs in a croissant and finish your breakfast with syrniki or a slice of cheesecake. The menu also features oatmeal and seasonal items. At the central location, you can sit by the window overlooking Rynok Square. Cheese Bakery locations are known for their aesthetically pleasing breakfasts, pastries, and house-made desserts.

- Address: 1 Halytska St.; there are also other locations in Lviv—it’s easy to find them on Google Maps.
- Hours: main location—9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.
- Average bill: approximately 190–485 UAH.

### Grand Cafe Leopolis

Grand Cafe Leopolis is located right inside Lviv City Hall. Its main morning specialty is the three-plate breakfast tower. Depending on the option, it features eggs, bacon, salmon, shrimp, salad, pastries, and a small dessert. The set may include rose lemonade or sparkling wine.

Each tower weighs about half a kilogram, so it’s more of a hearty breakfast or brunch than a quick coffee before work. If you don’t feel like ordering the whole tower, the menu also features pancakes, oatmeal, toast, and sweet dishes. Leopolis coffee is brewed in a cezve using rose water.

- Address: 1 Rynok Square.
- Hours: 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM; breakfast served from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- Average bill: approximately 215–450 UAH.

### Shalom

At Shalom, breakfasts have a distinct Israeli flair. People come here for shakshuka, hummus, labneh, and dishes packed with fresh vegetables. They also serve avocado toast on flaxseed bread, eggs Benedict with bacon or salmon, croque-madame, and sweet lazy dumplings.

Shalom opens at 8:00 a.m., so you can stop by right after the morning train arrives. The menu lets you choose a light breakfast with vegetables and hummus or a heartier option with eggs, bacon, or salmon. People tend to stay for a full breakfast here rather than just popping in for coffee.

- Address: 32 Brativ Rohatynetsiv St.
- Hours: daily from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM; breakfast served from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
- Average bill: approximately 250–500 UAH.

## What kinds of breakfasts can you find in Lviv?

To enjoy a delicious breakfast in Lviv, you don’t have to order the same Eggs Benedict every morning. The menus at Lviv’s establishments feature at least eight popular breakfast options:

- Classic Ukrainian. Syrniki, pancakes, varenyky, oatmeal, or other porridge. Modern versions include savory syrniki with salmon, brynza, seasonal berries, and signature sauces.
- English. Eggs, sausages, bacon, beans, mushrooms, vegetables, and toast. This is one of the most filling options, commonly found on restaurant and hotel menus.
- French-style. Croissant, brioche, butter, jam, and coffee. Cafés in Lviv often add savory fillings to these classics: you might find brioche with salmon, croissants with roast beef, or pancakes with a sweet sauce.
- Signature. Chefs put a new spin on familiar dishes with unusual fillings and presentations. This is how dishes like syrniki with fish, ciabatta with seafood, eggs with sweet potato pâté, or oatmeal with shrimp appear on the menu.
- Healthy. Avocado, granola, yogurt, fresh vegetables, salmon, and porridge. These options are usually lighter, but you can add an egg, cheese, or fish.
- Brunch. A late and more substantial breakfast, often served with a cocktail or sparkling wine. Brunch is perfect for a weekend when you want to linger in Lviv a little longer and take your time.
- Buffet. Guests choose their own dishes from a selection of prepared options. This format is convenient for families or groups with different preferences. The best-known example in our selection is Restauracja Bachevskych.
- All-day breakfast. Breakfast dishes can be ordered all day long. This format is a lifesaver for those who wake up closer to lunchtime or decide to take a stroll around the city first.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which restaurants in Lviv are open at 7 a.m. and serve breakfast?

If you’re looking for a place to have breakfast in Lviv at 7 a.m., check out Inshi Bistro on Soborna Square. It’s one of the few popular spots that opens at 7:00 a.m. Among the restaurants in our main selection, Shalom opens the earliest—at 8:00 a.m.

### Which restaurants in Lviv offer all-you-can-eat breakfasts?

The most famous option is Restauracja Bachevskych. For a fixed price, guests get access to a buffet featuring cold appetizers, eggs, pastries, fruit, and desserts. It’s best to check the terms and current price before your visit.

### Lviv restaurants offering buffet-style breakfasts

In addition to Restauracja Bachevskych, a buffet-style breakfast is served at Valentino restaurant at the Swiss Hotel. These offerings have specific morning hours and a fixed price.

### Which restaurants in Lviv offer breakfast delivery?

Grand Cafe Leopolis delivers select menu items and desserts via !FEST Delivery. You should also look for Cheese Bakery and Shalom on delivery apps. But keep in mind that delivery options may differ from the restaurant’s main menu.

### Which restaurants in Lviv offer a kids’ menu for breakfast?

Kooperativ Moloka offers select kids’ breakfast items: mini pancakes, eggs, vegetables, and homemade dairy dishes. At ROOF, Cheese Bakery, Grand Cafe Leopolis, and Bachevskych, it’s easy to find dishes for children even without a separate kids’ menu.

### Where can you find vegan breakfasts in Lviv?

At Shalom, you can order a breakfast with hummus, vegetables, and avocado, but it’s best to check the ingredients of the sauces and baked goods beforehand. Specialized vegan options are also available at the Tsukor chain.

### Where can you have breakfast on a terrace in Lviv?

For breakfast with a sweeping view of the city, choose ROOF. Restauracja Bachevskych has a green veranda and a conservatory, and at French Holidays you can sit at outdoor tables during warmer weather.

### Which restaurants serve breakfast all day long?

At French Holidays, the breakfast menu is available all day. All-day breakfast is also offered by some cafes in Lviv, including Tsukor, Papi, and Kooperativ Moloka.
