## Rooftop restaurants in Lviv: the best places with panoramic city views

For a romantic dinner, look first at ROOF or Valentino: both have cosy terraces, a calm atmosphere and suit a date. A family lunch is easy to plan at Tartufo Trattoria or 36 Po, and a party for a large group at Albertyn, Dakh or !FESTrepublic. For breakfast with a view, ROOF and 36 Po come first in this selection.

Restaurants in the centre of Lviv show the city from different angles. From Dakh, Cloud no.7 Panorama, 36 Po and Valentino you can see Lviv roofs, old façades and church towers. ROOF opens a wide panoramic view of the city, VEZHA lets you see Lviv from above, and TERRA Emily Restaurant is memorable for its terrace by the water.

The Lviv restaurants in this selection are easy to recognise by striking details: an aquarium several floors high, a tree growing inside the building, a brewery of their own, or a terrace above a former factory. Take into account not only the unique details, but the practical ones too: in the warm season, terrace opening hours, the music programme and access to the upper levels sometimes change, so it is worth checking these details in advance.

## ROOF — a cosy restaurant with a terrace in Lviv

Location. ROOF is in Eurohotel, close to the historic centre. The three-level restaurant complex starts on the 7th floor of the hotel, with the main hall and open areas above. Rynok Square is within walking distance.

What makes it special. For anyone who likes panoramic restaurants, ROOF is an excellent choice thanks to its spacious terrace and beautiful views of Lviv. The terrace has two levels: the upper deck has more open sky, while the lower level suits a quieter dinner with good food. From this height you can see the Town Hall and St. George’s Cathedral, and in the evening watch the Lviv roofs light up.

The menu combines Ukrainian and European dishes with the chef’s own dishes. There are seasonal plates made with local products, and with dinner you can choose wine, a cocktail or other drinks. In the morning guests are served breakfast. During the day the hall is filled with natural light. In the evening the Lviv panorama gradually lights up, so tables by the open part of the terrace are especially in demand later on.

Who it suits. ROOF is a great place for a long breakfast, a celebratory dinner, a date or a quiet meeting with friends. The different levels of the complex let you find a table in the open air or a cosy spot for a long conversation.

What to keep in mind. Tables with the best view are booked first. When you reserve, it is better to say which terrace level you want.

Opening hours. Daily from 09:00 to 00:00.

## The panoramic terrace of Valentino

Location. Valentino is on the roof of Swiss Hotel, near the Bernardine complex. It is a hotel rooftop terrace a few minutes’ walk from the heart of Lviv.

What makes it special. There are not many tables on the terrace, so even in the evening it keeps a calm mood. The panoramas of old Lviv look especially vivid before sunset. The wine list, refined dishes and restrained interior suit an unhurried dinner. The venue also works well for a business meeting when quiet and a central location matter.

Who it suits. This is an ideal place for a romantic dinner for two. For a small group, the terrace suits a quiet meeting with wine and a beautiful view.

What to keep in mind. There are not many tables with a view, so an evening reservation is better made in advance.

Opening hours. Daily from 08:00 to 22:00.

## Dakh restaurant in the very centre of Lviv

Location. Dakh is in a townhouse on Rynok Square. The entrance is in one of the buildings on the central square. After you climb to the upper level, the centre of Lviv opens up, with its towers, old façades and the movement below.

What makes it special. For those drawn to rooftop restaurants, there is an open terrace, signature cocktails and evening music. On some evenings there are DJ sets and special events. Sunset easily becomes the start of a long evening, and after dinner guests often stay for cocktails and the music programme. From the terrace you can clearly see the dense historic centre and the tops of the churches.

Who it suits. This is a place to meet friends, where dinner can be combined with the bar and the music programme.

What to keep in mind. On DJ-set and special-event days the terrace fills up faster.

Opening hours. Daily from 10:00 to 22:00.

## 36 Po

Location. The venue occupies a multi-level townhouse on Rynok Square. The panoramic terrace is on the top floor.

What makes it special. Among Lviv restaurants with a panorama, 36 Po stands out for a six-metre aquarium, its own brewery and an upper terrace. A retractable roof made at the Antonov plant covers it: in good weather the structure is opened, and on a cool day it protects the tables from wind and rain. Living plants complete the interior, and from the upper level you can see the roofs of old Lviv and Vysokyi Zamok hill.

Who it suits. It is convenient to come here with family or with guests of the city. The aquarium especially captivates children, and adults can order the house beer.

What to keep in mind. The building has several halls, so when booking you need to name the terrace specifically.

Opening hours. Monday to Thursday from 08:00 to 23:00, Friday to Sunday from 08:00 to 00:00.

## VEZHA panorama bar

Location. VEZHA is near Stryiskyi Park and occupies nine levels. From the upper floors there are views of the green part of the city and the distant districts.

What makes it special. From the upper levels you can see Stryiskyi Park, residential quarters and the hills on the outskirts of Lviv. Closer to evening people come here for the sunset, music and cocktails. The bar and lounge spaces are on different floors, so you can sit closer to the panorama or choose a separate table for a group.

Who it suits. VEZHA suits large groups, visitors to the city and anyone planning a celebration.

What to keep in mind. Access to the upper levels and the events programme are worth confirming when you book.

Opening hours. Daily from 12:00 to 22:00.

## The panoramic terrace of Albertyn

Location. Albertyn is at Zolota Street 2. The venue sits a little away from Rynok Square, but from the panoramic terrace the central part of the city is clearly visible.

What makes it special. The terrace is raised above the surrounding buildings, so the tables open onto a wide view of Lviv. The menu has dishes for a shared dinner and craft beer. There is enough space for a family lunch and for a large group.

Who it suits. Consider it for a birthday, a meeting with friends or a family dinner with a city view.

What to keep in mind. On warm evenings, tables closer to the panorama are better booked ahead.

Opening hours. Daily from 12:00 to 22:00.

## Tartufo Trattoria

Location. The restaurant is on the third floor of Forum Lviv. The summer terrace looks onto the modern part of the centre and the surrounding hills.

What makes it special. While adults have lunch, children can go to the play area or join a pizza-making workshop. Italian cuisine and light music suit a late breakfast or a family lunch. Large windows let you enjoy the views in the cooler season too.

Who it suits. Come here with children, or as a small group interested in Italian food who want to combine lunch with a walk through the central part of the city.

What to keep in mind. The summer terrace is open in warm weather, and children’s workshops run on a separate schedule.

Opening hours. Daily from 10:00 to 21:00.

## The café terrace at !FESTrepublic

Location. !FESTrepublic occupies the site of a former factory in Pidzamche. The terrace looks onto industrial buildings, the greenery of Znesinnia and the northern districts.

What makes it special. The space brings together a café, concert venues, markets and open courtyards. One day there is a concert, another a market, a festival or an open-air event. Metal structures, brick buildings and open courtyards recall the industrial past of the site.

Who it suits. Large groups and fans of live music will feel most at home. You can spend time with friends and then go on to a concert or festival.

What to keep in mind. On big concert days there are far more people, and the café’s hours can depend on the programme.

Opening hours. Roughly from 12:00 to 22:30; on major event days the schedule may change.

## Cloud no.7 Panorama

Location. Cloud no.7 Panorama is on Soborna Square, in the very heart of Lviv.

What makes it special. A tree grows through several levels of the restaurant, and the summer terrace opens a panoramic view of the city. The drinks list has signature cocktails and wines. In the evening there are music events, and at sunset the roofs of the surrounding buildings are lit with a romantic pink glow.

Who it suits. Come here for a date, a meeting with friends or a small celebration.

What to keep in mind. When booking, confirm whether your table will be in the hall or on the terrace.

Opening hours. Daily from 11:00 to 23:30.

## TERRA Emily Restaurant

Location. TERRA is on the grounds of Emily Resort in Vynnyky. The terrace sits by the lake and looks onto green slopes.

What makes it special. It is easy to stop at the restaurant after a walk around the resort. The seasonal menu suits an unhurried lunch or dinner after a day at Emily Resort. At the tables by the water you can take your time, and continue the walk after the visit.

Who it suits. A good choice for a family, a couple or a group that plans to spend time away from the central streets.

What to keep in mind. Allow extra time for the journey and a walk around the grounds.

Opening hours. Monday to Thursday from 13:00 to 23:00, Friday and weekends from 13:00 to 00:00.

## Frequently asked questions

### How to choose a restaurant with a summer terrace in Lviv?

First decide what matters most: the panorama, quiet, the food or the music. If you need a restaurant with a terrace in Lviv for a romantic dinner, consider ROOF or Valentino. For a large group, Dakh, Albertyn or !FESTrepublic work well, and families should look at 36 Po, Tartufo or TERRA.

Before booking, look at the restaurant menu and photos taken specifically from the terrace. They help you judge the view, where the tables sit and how far apart they are.

### Where to have breakfast on an open terrace?

ROOF opens at 09:00, and 36 Po at 08:00. The first offers a calm breakfast with a wide view, the second a bright multi-level space in the centre. Tartufo suits a late family breakfast.

### Which restaurants suit dinner with a view?

For a date, choose ROOF or Valentino. Dakh and Cloud no.7 Panorama offer a livelier atmosphere. 36 Po is worth considering for the terrace, the aquarium and the view of Rynok Square. The time of dinner also shapes the impression: before sunset the city is filled with soft light, and later the streets below gradually light up.

### Where to celebrate a birthday on a terrace?

For a small celebration, ROOF, Cloud no.7 Panorama or Valentino are a good fit. 36 Po has several halls and a terrace, Albertyn is convenient for a large group, and !FESTrepublic lets you combine the gathering with a concert.

When booking, check the terrace capacity, the menu format and the rules for bringing your own cake. Venues will offer several ways to arrange the tables. Places with the best view should be reserved in advance.
